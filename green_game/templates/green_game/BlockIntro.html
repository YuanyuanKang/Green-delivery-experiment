The formal game now begins

You have completed the practice rounds.

The formal game consists of 10 rounds. In every round, you will be randomly matched with another participant.

In each round, you will choose one of three Fulfilment Resource Commitment (FRC) levels:

  • Low FRC
  • Medium FRC
  • High FRC

Your FRC choice affects your Delivery Performance, Fulfilment Cost, market order allocation, carbon emissions, and ultimately your Profit.

Carbon policy

The formal game contains two carbon-price blocks:

  • Rounds 1–5: Low carbon price
  • Rounds 6–10: High carbon price

The current carbon price will always be displayed before you make your decision.

Important: Decisions and outcomes from the formal rounds count toward your experimental payoff.
