Practice before the formal game

You will first complete 2 practice rounds.

The practice rounds allow you to become familiar with:

  • choosing a Fulfilment Resource Commitment (FRC),
  • competing with another platform,
  • how Delivery Performance affects the allocation of market orders,
  • how Revenue, Fulfilment Cost and Carbon Cost determine your Profit.

The practice rounds follow the same decision and calculation rules as the formal game.

Important: Outcomes from the practice rounds do not count toward your formal experimental payoff.

After the two practice rounds, the formal game will begin.
